About

Addon Forge is an index of addons, plugins and packages for 3D software that are genuinely free to use, with a licence the project itself publishes.

What gets in

An entry needs six fields filled from the project's own pages: name, engine, licence, host, source link and the date it was checked. If a licence cannot be confirmed, the entry does not ship. That is why the counts are smaller than a scraper would report.

What stays out

Free trials, free tiers of paid tools, storefront listings priced at zero today, mirrors and reuploads. Every source link points at the project's own page.
